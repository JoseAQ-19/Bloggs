# 🦅 PROTOCOLO APEX PREDATOR USA — EDITORIAL STYLE BIBLE

## 1. AI & SaaS (Ref: TechCrunch + The Verge)

### GEO Conclusions (US Market)
- **BLUF Pattern**: TechCrunch starts with a one-sentence summary that is immediately citable by AI Overview. "OpenAI CEO Sam Altman addressed concerns about AI's environmental impact this week while speaking at an event hosted by The Indian Express."
- **Direct Quote Saturation**: Every 2-3 paragraphs contain a direct blockquote from the subject. This is Google's entity signal goldmine.
- **Cynical Title Case**: "Sam Altman Would Like to Remind You That Humans Use a Lot of Energy, Too" — note the editorial sarcasm baked into a factual headline.
- **Funding Math**: Always include Series, valuation, burn rate. "How Ricursive Intelligence raised $335M at a $4B valuation in 4 months."
- **Cross-linking as authority**: They weave links to their own previous coverage ("was in India for a major AI summit") for topical authority.

### Extracted Examples (TechCrunch, Feb 2026)
**Opening**: "OpenAI CEO Sam Altman addressed concerns about AI's environmental impact this week while speaking at an event hosted by The Indian Express."
**Quote block**: '"This is completely untrue, totally insane, no connection to reality," Altman said.'
**Title patterns**: "After all the hype, some AI experts don't think OpenClaw is all that exciting" / "Google VP warns that two types of AI startups may not survive"

## 2. Fitness (Ref: Stronger By Science + BarBend)

### GEO Conclusions (US Market)
- **Academic Depth**: SBS opens with a biochemistry primer, then says "I could spend 7,000 words recapping hundreds of studies... Instead, this article will explore the lesser-known aspects." This signals to Google: "This is THE definitive guide."
- **Inline PubMed Citations**: Every claim has an inline hyperlink to NCBI/PubMed. Not "studies show" — actual URLs.
- **Table of Contents**: Uses numbered anchor links for every H2 section. Google loves this for Featured Snippets.
- **Mechanism Diagrams**: Explains biological mechanisms (ATP → ADP → phosphocreatine cycle) at PhD level but digestible.
- **Anti-Broscience Signals**: "I could spend the next 7,000 words recapping hundreds of studies showing that creatine increases strength... that stopped being interesting 15 years ago."

### Extracted Examples (SBS)
**Opening**: "Without question, creatine is the gold standard by which all strength-related supplements are judged."
**Scientific precision**: "Compared to rest, the rate of ATP demand increases up to 1,000-fold during intense exercise."
**Anti-fluff signal**: "I could spend the next 7,000 words recapping hundreds of studies... Fortunately, I won't, because that stopped being interesting about 15 years ago."

## 3. Crypto (Ref: CoinDesk + The Block)

### GEO Conclusions (US Market)
- **Macro-First**: CoinDesk leads with macroeconomic context, not crypto prices: "President Donald Trump raised the global tariff rate to 15% despite a Supreme Court ruling..."
- **Cold Clinical Titles**: "Bitcoin see-saws around $68,000, DOGE, ETH slide as tariff uncertainty weighs on risk assets" — zero hype, pure data.
- **SEC/Policy as Content**: They have an entire "Policy" section citing SEC actions by name and bill name (CLARITY bill). Entity SEO at its finest.
- **Analyst Attribution**: "Bitcoin echoes 'late 2022' bear market bottom, K33 says" — always attribute the analysis to a named firm.
- **Financial Disclaimer**: Always present, always at the bottom.

### Extracted Examples (CoinDesk, Feb 2026)
**Title**: "Bitcoin price slips after Trump hikes worldwide tariff to 15% from 10% despite Supreme Court decision"
**Analyst citation**: "Bitcoin echoes 'late 2022' bear market bottom, K33 says"
**Sub-headline**: "Past capitulation waves have preceded sharp recoveries, but this time price is still fighting technical resistance even as ledger activity surges."

## 4. Tools / How-To (Ref: How-To Geek + Zapier Blog)

### GEO Conclusions (US Market)
- **Problem → Solution → Steps** structure dominates. The title IS the search query.
- **Bold UI elements**: Every button, menu item, or setting gets **bold** treatment.
- **Featured Snippet Bait**: First paragraph answers the title question in 1-2 sentences.
- **Numbered steps**: Always `1. 2. 3.` never bullet points for sequential actions.
- **Zero opinion**: Pure utility. No "I think..." or "In my experience..."

## 5. Viral / Think Pieces (Ref: Vox + The Atlantic Tech)

### GEO Conclusions (US Market)
- **Narrative Titles**: "The quiet collapse of..." / "AI agents could change your life — if they don't ruin it first" / "Gadgets are getting worse and more expensive at the same time"
- **Sub-deck as Hook**: Below the title, a punchy one-liner: "You've become increasingly replaceable." / "Blame AI." / "ChatGPT is boring compared to what comes next."
- **Think Piece DNA**: They never just report what happened. They analyze WHY it matters sociologically, economically, or philosophically.
- **Cultural Cross-pollination**: Vox connects tech to Gen Z behavior, nostalgia economics, geopolitics.

### Extracted Examples (Vox, Feb 2026)
**Title + Deck pairs**:
- "AI's threat to white-collar jobs just got more real" → "You've become increasingly replaceable."
- "Gadgets are getting worse and more expensive at the same time" → "Blame AI."
- "Claude has an 80-page 'soul document.' Is that enough to make it good?" → "Anthropic's philosopher reveals what went into the chatbot's moral education."
- "Why is Gen Z so obsessed with the 2010s?" → "2016 really wasn't that great. Why are we so nostalgic for it?"

## 6. YouTube / Creator Economy (Ref: Tubefilter + Dexerto)

### GEO Conclusions (US Market)
- **Creator-as-Business**: Tubefilter treats creators as businesses: "Snapchat pledges to 'unlock scalable creator revenue' with new Subscriptions product"
- **Industry Metrics**: RPM, concurrent viewership, ad revenue, subscriber growth rates — always quantified.
- **Platform Strategy**: They cover platform STRATEGY, not just news: "TikTok's pop music power... What can it do as a podcast?"
- **Quoted Headlines**: Titles often include direct quotes: '"In the Mix" shows off TikTok's pop music power'

### Extracted Examples (Tubefilter, Feb 2026)
**Title**: "New report dives into YouTube's 'pester power,' which converts kids' requests into purchases"
**Sub-deck**: "For kids in Generation Alpha, YouTube is far and away the most important platform, and it drives many purchases as a result."
**Platform analysis**: "Spotter is bringing its Showcase back to New York to build buzz around 'Creator TV'"
