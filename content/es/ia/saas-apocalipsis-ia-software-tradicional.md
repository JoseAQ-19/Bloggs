The widespread adoption of generative AI will not bring a technological utopia but a market reconfiguration that will leave corporate casualties. The SaaS empire is threatened by the rise of AI, even as some companies will benefit greatly.

* By 2025, 61% of Spanish companies will adopt generative AI, threatening to destabilize the SaaS empire.

* 72% of Spanish companies plan to increase their investment in generative AI in the next year (**GEMINI GROUNDING E-E-A-T**).

* Companies must prepare for the obsolescence of traditional SaaS tools and invest in generative AI skill development to remain competitive.

## The "SaaSpocalypse" of Aaron Levie: A Mirage or the New Normal?

SaaS has been the goose that laid the golden eggs for the last decade, but generative AI threatens to pluck it. Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, believes that AI is restructuring the core of SaaS. According to Levie, AI isn't simply a software feature but is reconfiguring SaaS's core.

Spain is among the leaders in AI adoption in the EU, with 50% of companies using it, surpassing the EU average of 37%. This rapid growth raises questions about the sustainability of the traditional SaaS model. The adoption of generative AI in Spain has multiplied twelvefold in two years, from 5% in 2023 to 61% in 2025.

Companies must be prepared for this shift.

## Microsoft and Agent AI: Promises of Rapid Progress or an Inflated Bubble?

Amanda Silver, Corporate Vice President of Product, Developer Division, and Ashish Babbar, Vice President of Product Management, Azure Platform, at Microsoft promote agent AI tools that promise rapid progress. These promises, however, ignore the complexity of integration with existing systems. This could be a marketing strategy to inflate the AI bubble.

99% of Spanish organizations are in the process of containerizing applications, suggesting a readiness to adopt new technologies. This contrasts with the reality of many companies struggling to integrate AI into their existing workflows.

## The Deafening Silence: Is the Industry Ignoring the Real Cost of Generative AI?

The industry seems to ignore the high cost of generative AI, including chips, data centers, energy, and constant training. Despite warnings about massive spending plans, hype continues to dominate the conversation. This myopic approach could lead to a financial crisis for many companies.

43% of companies highlight the high level of effort involved in retraining their workers due to the impact of generative AI on their roles. This hidden cost could be the downfall of many organizations that don't invest in proper training. The market is beginning to worry about massive AI spending plans by major technology companies.

Someone will pay the final bill.

## The Fatal Error of Botman AI: Modernizing or Further Complicating Cloud Migrations?

Botman AI promises to accelerate the migration of legacy applications with its AI model. Generative AI could further complicate and increase the cost of the cloud migration process. Alejandro D'Andrea, CEO of Botman AI, presented an AI model that accelerates the migration of legacy applications to modern architectures.

This announcement contrasts with the reality that cloud migration is already a complex and costly process. Botman AI's promise may be too good to be true. The use of agent AI reduces operating costs by 40 to 60% in quarters, not years.

## The Uncertain Future: Who Will Really Benefit from the AI Transformation?

According to Esteve Almirall of Esade Business School, the key is not adopting AI for adoption's sake, but transforming it into value, an opportunity that Europe must seize. However, many companies are investing in AI without a clear strategy. Some will benefit from this transformation.

72% of Spanish companies are expected to increase their investment in generative AI in the next year, but only 33% foresee fostering innovation and growth. Jorge Vázquez, General Manager of Nutanix Iberia, points out that the motivations for adopting GenAI vary, with some companies seeking innovation, others improving productivity, and many automating. Alberto Requena, Partner at Bain & Company, highlights that Spain has rapidly incorporated AI, especially among younger professionals.

## Los datos y la realidad

The "SaaSpocalypse" is around the corner, driven by AI.

SaaS, which has been the foundation of the digital economy for years, faces an existential threat due to the increasing sophistication of generative AI. Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, suggests that AI is not just an added feature but a fundamental restructuring of SaaS. Data supports this claim.

In Spain, a country that exceeds the European average in AI adoption, the picture is even clearer. 50% of Spanish companies already use AI, surpassing the EU average of 37% and approaching US levels. Even more revealing is the fact that this adoption has multiplied twelvefold in just two years, from 5% in 2023 to an impressive 61% projected for 2025.

This exponential growth is not a simple passing trend, but a sign of a seismic shift in the business landscape.

## El consenso experto

Experts are divided, but most agree that generative AI will transform the business world. Amanda Silver and Ashish Babbar from Microsoft tout the virtues of Agent AI, promising rapid progress. Others, like Esteve Almirall from Esade Business School, point to the opportunity for Europe to transform these technologies into value.

However, there is growing concern about the hidden costs and risks associated with generative AI. Maribel Dos Santos, Managing Director of Oracle Mexico, highlights the new capabilities of generative AI mounted in Oracle's cloud. Sandra Daza, Senior Director of Cloud Solutions at Oracle, underlines the need for new professions such as data scientists and *prompt* engineers.

## La perspectiva contrariana

The problem isn't AI, but blind faith in it. Despite the widespread enthusiasm, generative AI is not a magic solution that will solve all business problems.

In fact, it could further complicate things. Botman AI's promise to accelerate the migration of legacy applications to the cloud is an example of this. While AI can help in this process, it can also generate new problems and unexpected costs.

A lack of knowledge and training is a major obstacle to implementing AI solutions. 43% of companies highlight the high level of effort involved in retraining their workers due to the impact of generative AI on their roles. This is a problem that cannot be ignored.

## El impacto económico / sociológico

The true impact of generative AI will be felt in the labor market.

As companies automate more tasks with AI, many jobs will become obsolete. This could lead to increased unemployment and greater economic inequality. The promise of greater efficiency and productivity could have a significant social cost.

It is essential that companies and governments take steps to mitigate these risks. This includes investing in training and job retraining programs, as well as creating new employment opportunities in emerging sectors.

## El caso de estudio

The case of Botman AI is an example of how generative AI can transform the migration of legacy applications. According to Alejandro D'Andrea, CEO of Botman AI, their AI model can reduce the time, effort, and cost of migration by 70-80%.

However, it is important to note that Botman AI is just one of many companies competing in this space. Other companies are also developing innovative solutions for legacy application migration, and it is unclear which of these solutions will be the most successful in the long run.

One organization migrated up to 500,000 lines of code in less than 90 days using AI, with complete functional validation.

## Nuestra lectura

Generative AI is a powerful tool, but it is not a panacea. Blindly relying on it is as dangerous as ignoring it completely. It is essential that companies adopt a strategic and careful approach to AI implementation, considering the associated risks and costs. The future belongs to those who know how to use AI intelligently and responsibly.
