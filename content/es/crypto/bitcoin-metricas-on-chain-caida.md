## Whale Accumulation Under Scrutiny According to CryptoQuant

**CryptoQuant**, a blockchain analysis platform, has cast doubt on the common narrative of whale accumulation in Bitcoin. Julio Moreno of **CryptoQuant** suggests much of the publicly shared data regarding "whale accumulation" is distorted and related to cryptocurrency exchange activity rather than actual investor behavior. This alternative perspective challenges the idea that large institutions or individuals are accumulating Bitcoin. This could have significant implications for the cryptocurrency's future price.

According to Moreno, by filtering these distortions, the data indicates that large holders are still distributing Bitcoin. This information contradicts claims that whales are buying Bitcoin in large quantities, which is typically considered a bullish sign. Distribution by whales could indicate these investors are selling their holdings, potentially exerting downward pressure on Bitcoin's price. This analysis is based on blockchain data interpretation and may not fully reflect actual whale behavior.

The controversy over whale accumulation highlights the importance of due diligence in cryptocurrency analysis. Investors should be cautious when interpreting blockchain data and consider multiple perspectives before making investment decisions. The information provided by **CryptoQuant** is valuable for understanding potential market trends, but should not be considered a guarantee of future results. ## MVRV y SOPR: Señales confusas en el horizonte

As of February 13, 2026, the MVRV (Market Value to Realized Value) ratio hovered around 1.10, leading to speculation about an upcoming market bottom. Historically, MVRV values above 3.7 have indicated price highs, and values below 1 have indicated price lows. STH SOPR < 1 suggests the best buying opportunities. The MVRV is an on-chain metric that compares Bitcoin's market value to its realized value. Realized value is the price at which Bitcoins were last moved. A MVRV ratio of 1.10 suggests the market is slightly overvalued, but not to an extreme degree.

The SOPR (Spent Output Profit Ratio) indicates whether Bitcoin is being sold at a profit or loss by comparing the value when it is spent to the value when it is acquired. A SOPR above 1 indicates Bitcoins are being sold at a profit, while a SOPR below 1 indicates they are being sold at a loss. STH SOPR < 1 (short-term holder SOPR less than 1) suggests short-term holders are selling at a loss, which has historically been a signal of buying opportunities. The combination of these metrics provides a more complete view of the Bitcoin market.

While the MVRV and the SOPR can be helpful in identifying potential market turning points, they should not be considered infallible indicators. The cryptocurrency market is highly volatile and influenced by a wide range of factors. It is crucial that investors make their own informed decisions and not rely solely on these metrics. On-chain analysis, which includes studying the MVRV and the SOPR, has become an increasingly sophisticated discipline. Analysts are developing complex models to predict market movements. However, even the most advanced models have limitations and cannot accurately predict the future.
