## Let's Talk Straight

At **NovumWorld**, we are always looking for the next big story. But we value your time and ours. This is not the typical corporate contact page that no one reads. Here we speak human to human.

### Why Contact Us?

1.  **Leaks and Tips:** If you have confidential information about tech companies, AI startups, or crypto projects doing shady things, we are all ears. We use encrypted channels to protect our sources. Your anonymity is sacred to us.
2.  **Technical Feedback:** Found an error in a Novum Tools blueprint? Have a correction on a blockchain technical analysis? We appreciate accuracy. The community makes us better.
3.  **Real Collaborations:** If you have a genuine value proposition, a SaaS tool that really changes the game, or relevant academic research, we are open to listening and giving you a voice.

### What We Do NOT Want (Save your email)

*   **Generic Press Releases:** If you send the same thing to 500 outlets, don't send it here. We will delete it without reading.
*   **Spam "Guest Posting" Requests:** We do not sell cheap SEO links. Our domain authority is not for sale. If you want to write, it must be "Wall Street Journal" level content.
*   **Shitcoins and Projects without Whitepaper:** If your crypto project has no real utility, we are not interested.

### Response Time and SLA

We are a small but efficient team, distributed globally. We try to respond to all legitimate inquiries within **48 business hours**.

*   **Urgent (Leaks):** Maximum priority.
*   **Business:** 2-3 business days.
*   **Technical Support:** Depends on workload.

If you do not receive a response within that time, it is likely that your message has been automatically filtered by our anti-spam systems or, frankly, did not meet our editorial criteria. Don't take it personal, it's just business.
