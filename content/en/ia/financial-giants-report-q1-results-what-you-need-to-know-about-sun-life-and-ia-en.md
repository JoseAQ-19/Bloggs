## Pension Risk Transfer: A Booming Market with Hidden Risks

The Canadian pension risk transfer market's explosive growth to $11 billion in 2024, a 41% increase from 2023, signifies a crucial shift in how companies manage their long-term liabilities. This growth is not merely a statistic; it reflects a growing recognition among sponsors that transferring pension risks can alleviate financial burdens. Yet, this strategy is not without its perils.

Brent Simmons, Senior Vice President at Sun Life, noted that sponsors are increasingly concerned about reducing uncertainty in their pension plans. As market volatility rises, the focus on managing pension liabilities becomes paramount. However, this growth in the pension risk transfer market also introduces legal and operational risks that could backfire on companies.

The rising tide of pension risk transfers has led to a surge in related litigation, with claims asserting that these transfers breach fiduciary duties. Companies must be astutely aware of the potential for such legal challenges, which can significantly impact financial outcomes. The balance between mitigating risks and ensuring compliance with regulatory obligations is delicate and requires expert navigation.

As the market evolves, the potential for increased claims related to pension transfers cannot be overlooked. Companies that engage in these transactions must ensure that they are equipped to handle the complexities involved. Failure to do so could result in reputational damage and financial liabilities.
