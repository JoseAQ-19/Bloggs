### YouTube TV's New Integration: A Breakdown

The process for activating ESPN Unlimited on YouTube TV involves several steps:

1. Open the YouTube TV mobile app and select your profile.
2. Go to Settings and choose Sports.
3. Enable the ESPN connection and follow the ESPN activation process.
4. Accept the terms and conditions and sign into a free ESPN account.
5. A prompt will ask whether you want to share ESPN Fantasy data with YouTube TV before completing the link.

This integration implies that Google and ESPN will share a significant amount of viewership data between the companies, which may raise concerns for some sports fans regarding data privacy.

### What Does This Mean for Subscribers?

The addition of ESPN Unlimited to YouTube TV is a significant upgrade for sports and pro-wrestling fans. While YouTube TV already carried ESPN's linear channels, ESPN+ content and WWE's monthly premium events were not included. The new deal allows subscribers to access these premium features without incurring additional costs.
