## The $125M Question: Are KSI's Generous Splits Masking a Revenue Struggle?

The Sidemen, a collective of British YouTubers led by KSI (Olajide Olatunji), have built a sprawling business empire. As of 2025, their total business portfolio is valued at over £125 million, according to **Arthnova**. This includes ventures like Sidemen Clothing, Sides (a fast-food chain), XIX Vodka, and their increasingly popular YouTube channels. The group has leveraged its massive online following to create diverse revenue streams, moving beyond traditional advertising to build a comprehensive brand.

Eddie Hearn, a prominent boxing promoter, recently voiced his astonishment on a Sidemen podcast regarding KSI's decision to equally split earnings with the other members. This revelation prompts a crucial question: is this perceived generosity a sustainable business model, or does it conceal underlying financial pressures? KSI's individual brand is arguably the most recognizable within the group, leading to speculation about whether an equal split is truly equitable or if it's a strategy to maintain group cohesion amidst potential revenue disparities.

While an equal split might foster camaraderie and long-term collaboration, it also raises concerns about individual incentives and the allocation of resources. Does this arrangement stifle individual ambition and innovation, or does it create a more resilient and collaborative business environment? The answer may depend on the specific dynamics within the Sidemen, but Hearn's surprise underscores the unconventional nature of their financial arrangement in the high-stakes world of digital entertainment.

## The YouTube Facade: Why Sidemen Entertainment's Official Growth Hides Fair Play Concerns, according to [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/)

Sidemen Entertainment's official financial reports paint a picture of impressive growth. The company saw its net assets grow to over £7 million in the 12 months leading up to June 30, 2024, a significant turnaround from a deficit of £114,208 in the previous 18 months, according to **City A.M.** This surge in assets is attributed to the success of their various ventures, including their YouTube channels, merchandise, and live events. However, beneath this veneer of financial prosperity lie lingering questions about fair play and transparency.

One recurring concern, particularly among Reddit users, revolves around the actual prize money payouts in the Sidemen's challenge videos. Some users speculate that the advertised amounts are inflated for entertainment purposes, suggesting that the members may not actually receive the full sum. This perception of potential discrepancies erodes trust and could undermine the Sidemen's credibility with their audience. As one Reddit user questioned, "Does anyone know if the Sidemen actually get paid the money they win in the videos?" This sentiment highlights the importance of transparency in maintaining fan loyalty.

Furthermore, while the Sidemen's YouTube channels generate significant revenue, the long-term sustainability of relying solely on advertising revenue and brand partnerships is a valid concern. The digital landscape is constantly evolving, and platforms like YouTube can change their algorithms or policies at any time, potentially impacting the Sidemen's income. This reliance on external factors underscores the need for diversification and the development of their own intellectual property.

## Charity Match Smoke and Mirrors: What Jordan Schwarzenberger Isn't Telling You About Long-Term Viability

Jordan Schwarzenberger, the manager of the Sidemen at Arcade Media, emphasizes the importance of building the Sidemen's own brands and intellectual property to ensure long-term viability. He envisions the Sidemen as "Disney for Gen Z born from YouTube," signaling a shift away from short-term brand deals towards creating a sustainable media empire, as mentioned in [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/). Schwarzenberger's strategy aims to insulate the Sidemen from the volatile nature of online advertising and create a more resilient business model.

However, Schwarzenberger's vision might be overlooking some critical aspects of the Sidemen's current operations. While building their own brands is undoubtedly crucial, the Sidemen still heavily rely on brand partnerships and advertising revenue to fuel their various ventures. This reliance creates a potential conflict of interest, as the Sidemen may be incentivized to prioritize short-term profits over long-term brand building.

Moreover, the Sidemen's charitable endeavors, such as their annual charity football match, are often presented as purely altruistic efforts. While these events undoubtedly raise significant funds for worthy causes, they also serve as powerful marketing tools, enhancing the Sidemen's brand image and attracting new fans. There's nothing inherently wrong with this synergy, but it's important to acknowledge the dual purpose of these events and to scrutinize the transparency of the financial flows.

## Wembley's Hidden Costs: The Unacknowledged Expenses Eating Into Sidemen's Charitable Impact

The Sidemen's charity football matches at Wembley Stadium have become major events, drawing massive crowds and raising millions for charity. The 2025 match, for example, raised £4.7 million for charities including BBC's Children in Need, Bright Side, and M7 Education, according to **Tubefilter**. These events showcase the Sidemen's ability to leverage their influence for good and generate positive publicity.

However, behind the scenes, significant expenses are associated with organizing and executing these large-scale events. Stadium rental, security, staffing, marketing, and travel costs can quickly add up, potentially reducing the overall amount that is ultimately donated to charity. Reddit users have raised questions about the transparency of these expenses, wondering how they are covered before the remaining profits are donated. One user on Reddit implied the 2025 match was fixed for Josh to score.

This lack of transparency can erode public trust, especially if fans perceive that a significant portion of the funds raised are being used to cover expenses rather than directly benefiting the intended charities. To maintain their credibility, the Sidemen should provide a detailed breakdown of the expenses associated with their charity matches, demonstrating exactly how the money is being used and ensuring that the maximum amount possible reaches the beneficiaries.

The Sidemen have diversified their revenue streams through various ventures, including clothing, food, and subscription services. Schwarzenberger is actively trying to move away from brand deals and towards building their own brands, as previously cited. However, the success of these ventures is not guaranteed. If any of these ventures falter, it could impact the Sidemen's overall revenue and potentially create financial pressure within the group.

Moreover, the Sidemen's individual financial situations may vary, leading to potential conflicts of interest. Some members may have more successful individual ventures, while others may be more reliant on the group's collective revenue. This disparity could create tension within the group, especially if the equal split arrangement begins to feel unfair to those who are contributing more.
