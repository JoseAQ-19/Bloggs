## The Problem with Automatic Playlists

When YouTube Music's algorithm gets it wrong, it can be frustrating. Users may find that their playlists, which they've carefully curated over time, are being disrupted by unwanted track additions. These tracks may not fit the mood or vibe of the playlist, and can even break the flow of the music.

For example, a heavy metal workout playlist may veer into acoustic ballads, completely disrupting the high-energy vibe of the music. Similarly, a playlist that's meant to be relaxing and calming may be infused with upbeat and energetic tracks, ruining the ambiance.

This problem is not unique to YouTube Music. Other music streaming platforms, such as Spotify, also use algorithms to drive playlist additions. However, the issue is particularly pronounced on YouTube Music, where the algorithm is designed to prioritize discovery and exploration.

## A Secret Setting to Regain Control
