MrBeast's seemingly unstoppable reign as YouTube's golden child may be showing cracks, evidenced by a dramatic plunge in viewership and a barrage of ethical questions.

* MrBeast's YouTube views have plummeted by 50% in the last 12 months, dropping from approximately 900 million to 1.2 billion monthly views to 570 million.
* Mario Joos, Retention Director for popular YouTube channels, attributes the decline partially to a YouTube algorithm change that favors recent Shorts content.
* Creators and viewers may need to rethink reliance on YouTube as a primary income or entertainment source due to unpredictable algorithm shifts and ethical concerns.

## The Algorithm Apocalypse: Why YouTube's Shift is Hitting MrBeast Hard

YouTube's ever-shifting algorithm, often a source of frustration and anxiety for creators, seems to be playing a significant role in MrBeast's recent viewership decline. MrBeast's long-form views have dropped by 50% in the last 12 months, a startling figure for a channel that once seemed impervious to the platform's whims. This drop is not merely a statistical anomaly; it represents a potential paradigm shift in how YouTube rewards and promotes content, with implications that extend far beyond a single creator.

The core issue appears to be YouTube's increasing emphasis on short-form video, particularly Shorts. As Retention Director for popular YouTube channels like MrBeast, Stokes Twins, and Alan's Universe, Mario Joos has observed firsthand the algorithm's preference for recency. According to Joos, YouTube is prioritizing newer Shorts content, potentially at the expense of older, established videos—even those from mega-channels like MrBeast. This shift disproportionately affects creators who rely on revenue from long-form content, as Shorts generally command lower CPMs (cost per mille) and RPMs (revenue per mille) compared to traditional YouTube videos.

This algorithmic pivot isn't just about views; it's about monetization. While MrBeast undeniably benefits from the vast reach of YouTube, his business model hinges on high CPMs generated by longer videos that attract premium advertisers. If the algorithm is now favoring content that generates less revenue per view, even a slight dip in viewership can translate into a significant reduction in overall income. The question then becomes: Can MrBeast adapt to this new landscape, or will the algorithm's unpredictable nature continue to erode his channel's earning potential.

## Feastables' Ethical Chocolate Facade: Cracks in the MrBeast Empire, according to [Social Blade](https://socialblade.com/)

Beyond the algorithmic challenges, MrBeast's brand is facing scrutiny regarding the ethics of his chocolate company, Feastables. While positioned as an ethical alternative to traditional chocolate, Feastables has faced serious accusations, including its removal from slavefreechocolate.org's list of Ethical Chocolate Companies. This delisting, regardless of the specific reasons behind it, raises uncomfortable questions about the true nature of Feastables' sourcing and production practices.

The chocolate industry has long been plagued by issues of child labor and exploitation, a reality that MrBeast himself has acknowledged. He stated that Feastables was created as an ethical alternative to traditional chocolate brands, citing over 1.5 million children in illegal labor on cocoa farms. However, the mere existence of Feastables does not automatically absolve it from ethical concerns. The company's supply chain, sourcing practices, and labor standards are all subject to intense scrutiny, especially given MrBeast's public commitment to ethical business practices.

While Feastables now sources 100% of its cocoa from Fairtrade certified cooperatives, this move doesn't necessarily guarantee complete ethical purity. Fairtrade certification provides a framework for responsible sourcing, but it's not a foolproof system. Critics argue that Fairtrade standards can sometimes be inadequate and that independent audits are necessary to ensure true ethical compliance. The fact that [slavefreechocolate.org](https://www.slavefreechocolate.org), an organization dedicated to combating child labor in the chocolate industry, removed Feastables from its list raises serious doubts about the effectiveness of its ethical claims, demanding increased transparency and accountability from the MrBeast team.

## The Shortsighted Strategy: Why MrBeast's Focus May Be Backfiring

MrBeast's strategic pivot toward short-form content, driven by YouTube's algorithmic push, may be a case of chasing short-term gains at the expense of long-term brand equity. While Shorts offer a quick and easy way to boost views and subscriber counts, they often lack the depth and engagement of long-form videos, potentially diluting the overall MrBeast brand.

Reed Duchscher, Ex-Manager of MrBeast, stated that platforms like YouTube and TikTok have evolved, making it more difficult for creators to reach a mass audience. According to Duchscher, algorithms now keep users within specific verticals, making it harder for new creators to become the next MrBeast. This means that relying solely on the YouTube algorithm, particularly its current focus on Shorts, may not be a sustainable strategy for long-term growth and influence.

The relentless pursuit of viral moments and fleeting trends can distract from building a loyal and engaged community. MrBeast's early success was built on elaborate stunts, generous giveaways, and a unique brand of chaotic energy – all of which are difficult to replicate in the compressed format of a Short. By prioritizing quantity over quality and chasing the algorithm's favor, MrBeast risks alienating his core audience and sacrificing the very qualities that made him stand out in the first place.

The pursuit of viral content should never come at the expense of human safety or dignity. If MrBeast's brand is to remain synonymous with generosity and positive impact, he must prioritize ethical considerations above all else. A single incident of exploitation or negligence can irreparably damage his reputation and erode the trust of his audience.

## The Future of Fandom: MrBeast's Faltering Grip on the Next Generation

MrBeast's subscriber growth, once a seemingly unstoppable force, is showing signs of deceleration. In early February 2025, MrBeast was gaining 17+ million subscribers per month, which has dropped to 6 million a month, signaling a potential plateau in his ability to capture the attention of new audiences. This slowdown isn't necessarily a sign of imminent collapse, but it does suggest that MrBeast's dominance is not guaranteed.

The digital landscape is constantly evolving, with new platforms and creators emerging to challenge the established order. Generation Alpha, the demographic that forms a significant portion of MrBeast's viewership, is notoriously fickle and easily distracted. What resonates with them today may be forgotten tomorrow. To maintain his relevance and appeal, MrBeast must continuously adapt and innovate, not just by churning out more content, but by fostering deeper connections with his audience.

The future of fandom lies in authenticity and engagement. Viewers are increasingly drawn to creators who are transparent, relatable, and genuinely invested in their communities. MrBeast's brand, while built on generosity and spectacle, must also cultivate a sense of genuine connection with his audience. He needs to find ways to foster meaningful interactions, solicit feedback, and empower his viewers to become active participants in his creative process.
