## The Moldy Pizza Incident at Lunchly

The internet was briefly captivated when Rosanna Pansino, the baker and YouTube personality with 14.8 million subscribers, opened a Lunchly pizza kit and found mold. The incident, captured on video, sparked concerns about the quality control processes behind MrBeast's venture into the food industry.

Pansino herself took to X to express her dismay: "This is disgusting. I was filming a video comparing Lunchables to Lunchly and was shocked when I opened 'The Pizza' Lunchly. The cheese had MOLD!" This public declaration amplified the issue, turning a potential oversight into a scandal that MrBeast's team struggled to contain.

The Lunchly brand, a collaboration between MrBeast, Logan Paul, and KSI, aimed to disrupt the ready-to-eat meal market, but this incident exposed vulnerabilities in their supply chain. The immediate fallout included negative publicity and consumer skepticism, threatening to undermine the brand's initial promise. Social media fame can outpace operational competence.

## Prime Hydration's Plummeting Popularity, according to [Social Blade](https://socialblade.com/)

The moldy pizza incident highlights a broader trend of potential overextension within MrBeast's expanding business empire. One concerning indicator is the dramatic decline in revenue for Prime Hydration, a beverage company heavily promoted by Logan Paul and KSI.

Prime Hydration's sales have plummeted approximately 70% from $131 million in 2023 to $38 million in 2024. Some outlets have claimed Prime's year-over-year decline could be as steep as 90%.

This sharp decline raises questions about the long-term sustainability of celebrity-driven brands, especially when they face increasing competition and scrutiny. Initial hype and influencer marketing can only carry a product so far, and sustained success requires a solid foundation of quality, value, and consumer trust. In 2023, Prime's sales hit approximately $1.2 billion.

MrBeast, with his focus on viral content and ambitious philanthropic projects, may be spreading himself too thin, potentially overlooking critical aspects of product development, manufacturing, and quality control. Feastables generated $250 million in sales and $20 million in profit in 2024, but it's unclear if MrBeast can maintain the same level of dedication to quality. He aims to triple 2024 revenue by 2026.

## The Limits of MrBeast's Brand Power: Food Safety

MrBeast has a level of brand power that most creators can only dream of, but even that power has its limits. MrBeast's company, Feastables, was sued for its website being inaccessible to the blind, violating the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

The suit alleges that visually impaired customers are unable to navigate or make purchases on the website, which is a violation of federal law. This lawsuit underscores the potential pitfalls of rapid expansion and the importance of ensuring accessibility and inclusivity in all aspects of a business.

The suit could cost millions.

This is where Feastables, Prime, and Lunchly become cautionary tales. Celebrity endorsements and viral marketing can drive initial sales, but they cannot mask fundamental flaws in product quality, safety, or accessibility. When consumers feel misled or experience issues like mold contamination, the backlash can be swift and severe, eroding brand loyalty and long-term profitability. The most successful brands prioritize consumer trust above all else, recognizing that a single misstep can have lasting consequences.

## Lunchly's Liability: Potential Lawsuits

The mold contamination in Lunchly products raises serious concerns about potential legal liabilities. With more than 10 consumers reporting issues to the FDA, the company faces the risk of lawsuits based on negligence, breach of contract, or premises liability. Punitive damages may be awarded in mold cases to punish negligence.

These lawsuits can be costly and time-consuming, diverting resources away from product development and marketing. They can also damage the company's reputation and erode consumer trust, making it more difficult to attract new customers and retain existing ones. MrBeast, Logan Paul, and KSI's Lunchly meal brand faced FDA complaints over mold.

[Elite Mold Services](https://elitemoldservices.com/) states that long-term mold exposure can contribute to chronic health issues, which further increases the risk of a lawsuit for Lunchly and its parent companies. The company's failure to ensure the safety of its products could result in significant financial losses and reputational damage.
