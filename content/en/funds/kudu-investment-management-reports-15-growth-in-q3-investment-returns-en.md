## Comparative Analysis of Funds

Kudu Investment Management's performance can be contextualized against its peers. Over the past year, Kudu has achieved a 15% return, outpacing the industry average of approximately 10%. This trend is consistent over three years, where Kudu's annualized return stands at 12%, compared to the sector's 9%. Over a five-year horizon, Kudu has demonstrated a 10% return versus the industry average of 8%.

Key performance metrics include:

- **Volatility**: Kudu maintains a volatility measure of 8%, lower than the industry average of 10%.
- **Sharpe Ratio**: The Sharpe ratio for Kudu is currently at 1.5, indicating a favorable risk-adjusted return compared to an industry average of 1.2.
- **Expense Ratio**: Kudu’s expense ratio is set at 0.75%, which is competitive against an average of 1.0% across similar funds.

These details reveal Kudu's strong position within the investment management sector, driven by strategic asset allocation and disciplined risk management.
