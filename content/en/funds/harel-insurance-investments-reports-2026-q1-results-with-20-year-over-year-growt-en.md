## Financial Performance Overview

Harel's financial performance showcases robust metrics that reflect its strategic decisions and market positioning. The company reported total revenues of NIS 1.2 billion for the first quarter of 2026, an increase from NIS 1 billion in the same period last year. This growth was driven by increased premiums collected across its insurance products and higher investment income, which rose due to favorable market conditions.

In terms of profitability, Harel's operating profit reached NIS 300 million, a significant increase compared to NIS 250 million in Q1 2025, marking a 20% year-over-year improvement. The net profit also saw a notable rise of 18%, reaching NIS 200 million. This performance underscores the effectiveness of Harel's strategic initiatives, including expanding its customer base and enhancing service delivery.
