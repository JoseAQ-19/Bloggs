### Risks and Contrarian Perspectives

Despite the positive outlook, there are inherent risks associated with the PGIM Jennison US Core Equity ETF. Active management carries the risk of underperformance relative to passive strategies, especially in prolonged bull markets where index-based funds often outperform. Additionally, market volatility can affect the performance of actively managed funds, particularly those that do not adhere to a strict investment discipline.

Some analysts argue that the increasing popularity of passive investment strategies may pose a challenge to actively managed funds. “As more investors lean towards low-cost index funds, the space for active management could shrink,” warns Tom Brown, a financial strategist at **CNMV**.
