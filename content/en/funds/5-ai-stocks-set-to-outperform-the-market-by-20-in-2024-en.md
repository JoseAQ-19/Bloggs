## Risks and Contrarian Perspectives 

While the growth potential of AI stocks is significant, investors must also navigate inherent risks. Regulatory scrutiny regarding data privacy and AI ethics is on the rise, potentially impacting company operations. Additionally, the volatility associated with emerging technologies may deter conservative investors. 

Furthermore, the high valuation ratios of many AI stocks raise concerns about unsustainable growth. Market corrections could lead to substantial declines in stock prices. As noted by **Morningstar**, “Not all AI companies will succeed; investors must perform due diligence to avoid potential pitfalls.”

### What are the top AI stocks to consider for 2024?
