## Risks Associated with High SpaceX Allocations

Investing in funds with heavy allocations to SpaceX is not without its risks. The aerospace industry is notoriously volatile, influenced by technological advancements, regulatory changes, and market competition. For instance, SpaceX faces competition from established players like Boeing and new entrants such as Blue Origin. Any setbacks in launch schedules or regulatory hurdles can adversely affect the stock price and, by extension, the ETFs that hold significant shares.

Additionally, the high concentration of funds in a single stock raises concerns about liquidity. In times of market distress, the ability to sell shares without impacting the price could be severely hampered, exposing investors to greater risk.
