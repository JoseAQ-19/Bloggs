## Real User FAQs

### What are the key indicators of a good mutual fund?
Investors should look for funds with a strong track record of performance, low expense ratios, and a high Sharpe ratio, which indicates better risk-adjusted returns.

### How often should I review my mutual fund investments?
It is advisable to review your investments at least annually to ensure they align with your financial goals, risk tolerance, and market conditions.

### What should I do if my fund underperforms?
Consider evaluating the fund's management team, investment strategy, and market conditions. If concerns persist, it may be prudent to look for alternatives.

### How do expense ratios affect my returns?
Higher expense ratios can significantly erode your returns over time. A lower expense ratio typically means more of your investment is working for you, resulting in higher net returns.

### Are actively managed funds worth the cost?
While actively managed funds can offer unique strategies and potentially higher returns, they often come with higher fees. Evaluating their performance against passive alternatives is essential for making an informed decision.
