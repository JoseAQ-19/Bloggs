## Real User FAQs

Investors often have questions when considering new investment products, especially those like interval funds that deviate from traditional models. Below are frequently asked questions based on real investor concerns:

### What are interval funds and how do they work?
Interval funds are a type of closed-end fund that offers periodic liquidity to investors, allowing them to redeem shares at set intervals rather than daily. This structure enables these funds to invest in less liquid assets, potentially providing higher returns.

### What are the risks associated with investing in interval funds?
Investors face several risks, including limited liquidity, potential market volatility, and the challenges associated with the underlying less liquid assets. It is essential to consider these factors before investing.

### How do interval funds compare to traditional mutual funds?
While traditional mutual funds offer daily liquidity, interval funds provide periodic liquidity, often investing in less liquid assets that may yield higher returns. The choice between them depends on an investor's liquidity needs and risk tolerance.

### Are interval funds suitable for all investors?
Interval funds may be more suitable for long-term investors who can tolerate reduced liquidity in exchange for potentially higher returns. It is crucial for investors to assess their individual investment goals before proceeding.

### How do fees impact the overall performance of interval funds?
Fees can significantly impact net returns. Investors should consider the total expense ratio and how it compares to the expected performance of the fund when evaluating investment options.
