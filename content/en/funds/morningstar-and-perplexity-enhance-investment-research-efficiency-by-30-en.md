#### Are low-cost funds always better?
While lower fees generally correlate with better performance, it is essential to evaluate fund objectives and strategies, as not all low-cost funds outperform.

#### How do volatility and Sharpe ratios affect my investment decisions?
Volatility indicates risk, while the Sharpe ratio helps assess risk-adjusted return. Both are crucial for understanding the trade-offs in your investment portfolio.

#### What should I consider when evaluating tech stocks?
Investors should assess fundamentals, market conditions, and potential for sustainable growth, as well as be cautious of overvaluation.

#### Is automated trading a reliable strategy?
Automated trading can enhance efficiency but introduces risks related to algorithmic decision-making and market anomalies.
