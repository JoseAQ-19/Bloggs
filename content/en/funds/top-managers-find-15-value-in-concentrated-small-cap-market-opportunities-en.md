## Contrarian Views and Risks

While the small-cap market appears ripe for investment, it is essential to consider the inherent risks. Economic downturns can disproportionately affect smaller firms due to their limited resources and market presence. According to a report by **CNMV**, “The market’s volatility can lead to substantial short-term losses, especially if macroeconomic conditions deteriorate.” Additionally, liquidity concerns may pose challenges, as smaller companies often lack the trading volume needed to absorb large buy or sell orders without significantly affecting stock prices.

Furthermore, investors should be cautious of concentration risks within portfolios that emphasize small-cap stocks. A fund heavily weighted in a few successful small-cap stocks may outperform, but the potential for substantial losses in case of underperformance from those stocks is equally significant.
