### Fee Structure Comparison

Fee structures are a critical consideration for investors. The average Total Expense Ratio (TER) for actively managed healthcare funds is around 1.2%, whereas technology funds average about 1.5%. In contrast, index funds tracking the broader market typically have a TER of only 0.3%. 

When evaluating the impact of these fees on performance, consider that a 1% difference in fees can significantly erode returns over the long term. For example, an initial investment of $10,000 in a fund with a 5% annual return over 30 years would grow to approximately $43,219 with a 0.3% fee but only $28,346 with a 1.5% fee. This underscores the importance of fee awareness when constructing a portfolio.
