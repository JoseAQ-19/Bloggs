## Comparative Analysis of Morningstar Indexes

The performance of Morningstar indexes post-integration shows promising trends. Over the past one, three, and five years, various Morningstar indexes have displayed competitive returns relative to broader market benchmarks like the S&P 500. 

- **1-Year Performance**: Morningstar's U.S. Market Index has returned 15.3%, while the S&P 500 posted a return of 14.1%.
- **3-Year Performance**: The Morningstar U.S. Growth Index outperformed with a 22% return, compared to the S&P 500's 18%.
- **5-Year Performance**: Morningstar's U.S. Value Index yielded 10.5%, surpassing the S&P 500's 9.3%.

When considering volatility, Morningstar indexes have maintained a Sharpe ratio of 1.2 over the last three years, indicating a favorable risk-adjusted performance. In terms of fees, the average expense ratio reduction from 0.12% to 0.08% represents a significant decrease, enhancing net investor returns.
