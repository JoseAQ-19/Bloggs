## Comparative Analysis of TRUF and TRUH

In assessing the performance metrics of TRUF and TRUH, a clear differentiation emerges. Over the past year, TRUF has outperformed with an **18% return**, compared to TRUH's **14%**. This performance is reflective of the broader recovery in financial markets, driven by rising interest rates and strong corporate earnings.

### Performance Metrics

- **1-Year Return**: TRUF at 18%, TRUH at 14%
- **3-Year Return**: TRUF at 15%, TRUH at 12%
- **5-Year Return**: TRUF at 10%, TRUH at 9%
- **Volatility**: TRUF at 12.0%, TRUH at 11.5%
- **Sharpe Ratio**: Both ETFs reveal a Sharpe ratio of approximately 1.2, indicating decent risk-adjusted returns.

The total expense ratio (TER) for TRUF stands at **0.40%**, while TRUH maintains a **0.45%** TER. When comparing the fees to performance, TRUF's lower expense ratio provides a slight edge, enhancing net returns over time.
