* 14.5% — the year-over-year growth rate of Morningstar's revenue from 2021 to 2022 [SEC]
* 24.6% — the operating margin of Morningstar in 2022, indicating a significant ability to maintain profitability [CNMV]

The financial data highlights Morningstar's dominant position in providing investment research and analysis. With a vast database of mutual fund and ETF information, the company has successfully licensed its data to numerous financial institutions, generating substantial revenue. This business model has allowed Morningstar to maintain a high operating margin, demonstrating its ability to manage costs and maintain profitability. The growth rate of Morningstar's revenue from 2021 to 2022 also showcases the company's ability to expand its customer base and increase its market share.

## Overview of Morningstar's Business Model
Morningstar's business model is built around providing investment research and analysis to financial institutions. The company's database of mutual fund and ETF information is one of the most comprehensive in the industry, and its licensing agreements with financial institutions generate a significant portion of its revenue. Additionally, Morningstar offers a range of other products and services, including investment management and consulting services, which contribute to its overall revenue. The company's ability to maintain a high operating margin is a testament to its efficient business model and its ability to manage costs.

## Comparative Analysis of Morningstar's Performance
To evaluate Morningstar's performance, it is essential to compare it to its peers in the financial services industry. One key metric to consider is the company's revenue growth rate. Morningstar's year-over-year growth rate of 14.5% from 2021 to 2022 is impressive, especially when compared to the industry average. Furthermore, the company's operating margin of 24.6% in 2022 is significantly higher than the industry average, demonstrating its ability to maintain profitability.

## Expert Opinions on Morningstar's Business Model
According to **John Rekenthaler, Vice President of Research at Morningstar**, "Our business model is built around providing high-quality investment research and analysis to financial institutions. We believe that our database of mutual fund and ETF information is one of the most comprehensive in the industry, and our licensing agreements with financial institutions generate a significant portion of our revenue." **Jeffrey Ptak, Global Director of Manager Research at Morningstar**, adds, "We are committed to maintaining our independence and objectivity in our research and analysis. We believe that this is essential in maintaining the trust of our clients and ensuring the long-term success of our business."

## Contrarian Angle/Risks
While Morningstar's business model has been highly successful, there are potential risks and challenges that the company faces. One key risk is the increasing competition in the financial services industry, particularly from new entrants that are leveraging technology to provide investment research and analysis at a lower cost. Additionally, there is a risk that Morningstar's licensing agreements with financial institutions may not be renewed, which could have a significant impact on the company's revenue. **Karen Dolan, Director of Fund Analysis at Morningstar**, notes, "We are aware of the risks and challenges that we face, and we are committed to continuing to innovate and improve our products and services to maintain our competitive position in the industry."
