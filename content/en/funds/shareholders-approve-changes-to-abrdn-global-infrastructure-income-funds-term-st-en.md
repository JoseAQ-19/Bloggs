## Risks and Contrarian Perspectives

While the approval has been met with optimism, it is essential to consider the potential risks associated with this strategic shift. The removal of a set term structure could lead to increased volatility in the fund's investment strategy. Without defined timelines, there may be a tendency to chase short-term gains, which can detract from long-term stability. 

Moreover, infrastructure investments are inherently cyclical, and the fund's focus on yield may expose it to interest rate risks, particularly in a rising rate environment. Investors should be aware that while the fund aims to provide attractive income, it may have to navigate fluctuating economic conditions.
