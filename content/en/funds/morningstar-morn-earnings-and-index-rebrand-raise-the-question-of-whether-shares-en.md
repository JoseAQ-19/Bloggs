Morningstar's decision to rebrand its indexes is a strategic move aimed at expanding its global benchmarks business. This move is expected to increase Morningstar's visibility and credibility in the indexing space, and will likely attract new customers to its platform. According to **John Rekenthaler**, Vice President of Research at **Morningstar**, "The rebranding of our indexes is a significant milestone for our company, and we believe it will have a positive impact on our financial performance."
