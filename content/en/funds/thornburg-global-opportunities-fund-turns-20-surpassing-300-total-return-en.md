## Performance Analysis

Over the past one, three, and five years, TGOFX has delivered annualized returns of 15.3%, 10.5%, and 8.8%, respectively. In comparison, its benchmark has returned 12.4%, 8.0%, and 7.0% over the same periods. 

The fund's Sharpe ratio, measuring risk-adjusted returns, stands at 1.25, indicating that it has provided a solid return for the level of risk taken. This ratio is essential for investors looking to assess the efficiency of their investment in comparison to the inherent volatility of the market.

Volatility, measured by standard deviation, is another critical aspect of TGOFX's profile. The fund exhibits a three-year standard deviation of 10.2%, which is lower than the benchmark's 11.5%. This lower volatility combined with its superior performance highlights the fund's effectiveness in managing risk while pursuing growth.

The expense ratio of 1.02% may seem higher than some passive investment options, but when contrasted with the fund's performance, it remains competitive, especially given its active management approach. The management team employs a rigorous research process, focusing on quality growth companies with strong fundamentals and sustainable competitive advantages.

## Risk Factors and Contrarian Perspectives

Despite the fund's strong performance, potential investors should consider the inherent risks associated with a globally diversified investment strategy. The fund's exposure to international markets may introduce geopolitical risks and currency fluctuations, which could adversely affect returns. Additionally, the fund's active management style may not always outperform passive benchmarks, particularly in strongly trending markets.

Furthermore, as interest rates potentially rise in response to inflationary pressures, growth-oriented funds like TGOFX may face headwinds. Higher rates generally favor value stocks over growth stocks, which could impact the fund's performance if the economic environment shifts.
