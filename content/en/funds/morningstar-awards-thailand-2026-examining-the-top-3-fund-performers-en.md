## Top 3 Fund Performers: A Comparative Analysis

### Kasikorn Thai Equity Fund

The Kasikorn Thai Equity Fund has been a consistent performer in the Thailand fund market, with a 1-year return of 10.35%. The fund's investment strategy focuses on long-term growth, with a portfolio that is diversified across various sectors, including finance, consumer goods, and industrials.

### Krungsri Equity Fund

The Krungsri Equity Fund has a 3-year return of 8.12%, making it one of the top performers in the Thailand fund market. The fund's investment strategy focuses on identifying undervalued stocks with strong growth potential.

According to [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/profile/company/1255023D:TH), the fund has a 5-year beta of 0.92, indicating its relatively low sensitivity to market movements. The fund's expense ratio is 1.50%, which is slightly higher than the category average.

### SCB Equity Fund

The SCB Equity Fund has a 5-year return of 6.85%, making it a consistent performer in the Thailand fund market. The fund's investment strategy focuses on long-term growth, with a portfolio that is diversified across various sectors, including finance, consumer goods, and industrials.
