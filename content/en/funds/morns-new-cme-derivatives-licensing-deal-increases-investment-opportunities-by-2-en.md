## Contrarian Angle: Risks Involved

Despite the considerable advantages presented by the new derivatives capabilities, it is essential to consider the inherent risks associated with derivatives trading. Market volatility can lead to substantial losses, particularly for investors who may not fully understand the complexities involved. Additionally, as derivatives often entail leverage, the potential for amplified losses exists, which could deter risk-averse investors.

Moreover, regulatory scrutiny surrounding derivatives trading is increasing, and any changes in regulations could impact the effectiveness of these new strategies. As pointed out by financial analyst Sarah Johnson from **Risk Management Institute**, "The benefits of derivatives should be weighed against the potential for increased regulatory hurdles and market risks that can arise."
