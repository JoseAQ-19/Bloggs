## Comparative Performance Analysis

When evaluating PGIM Real Estate Fund against its peers, the performance metrics paint a compelling picture. Over the past year, the fund has reported a return of 10.5%, outperforming the average return of 8.2% for its peers. Over a three-year horizon, PGIM has delivered a 7.9% annualized return, compared to the peer average of 6.5%. Its five-year annualized return stands at 8.7%, exceeding the industry benchmark of 7.4%. 

In terms of volatility, the fund maintains a standard deviation of 12.5%, which is lower than the peer average of 14.3%. The Sharpe ratio, a measure of risk-adjusted return, comes in at 0.85, indicating a favorable risk-return profile compared to the industry average of 0.73. 

The fund's total expense ratio (TER) of 1.25% is competitive, given that the average for similar funds hovers around 1.50%. This fee structure allows for a more favorable net return to investors, particularly in an environment where cost-sensitive strategies are increasingly relevant.
