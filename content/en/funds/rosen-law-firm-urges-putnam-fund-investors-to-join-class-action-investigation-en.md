## Comparative Performance Analysis of Putnam Funds

When evaluating the performance of Putnam funds over various time horizons, stark discrepancies emerge. In the past year, the average Putnam fund has yielded a return of approximately -2.4%, compared to a positive 7.1% from the S&P 500. Over three years, Putnam funds have shown an average return of only 2.5%, while the benchmark has returned 10.8%. The five-year horizon reveals a similar trend, with Putnam funds averaging 3.9%, significantly trailing the 12.6% return from the broader market.

In terms of risk-adjusted returns, Putnam funds also display concerning volatility metrics. The standard deviation of returns for their funds stands at 15.6%, suggesting higher volatility than the industry average of 12.9%. The Sharpe ratio, a key indicator of risk-adjusted performance, for Putnam funds is a mere 0.24, indicative of poor compensation for the risks taken, especially when compared to a Sharpe ratio of 0.90 for top-performing funds in similar categories.
