## Stock Selection Criteria

Selecting stocks capable of outperforming the market requires a multi-dimensional approach. Key criteria include:

1. **Fundamental Strength**: Companies with robust financial statements, low debt levels, and consistent earnings growth typically have a competitive advantage.
2. **Market Position**: Firms that dominate their sectors or have unique offerings can weather economic downturns better than their peers.
3. **Valuation Metrics**: Stocks trading at reasonable price-to-earnings (P/E) ratios relative to their historical averages may present attractive entry points.
