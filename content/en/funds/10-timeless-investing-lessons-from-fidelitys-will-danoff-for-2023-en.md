## Performance Metrics: A Closer Look at Fidelity Contrafund

Fidelity Contrafund (FCNTX) has demonstrated robust performance metrics, making it a focal point for investors. Over the past year, the fund achieved a return of approximately 18.3%, while the three-year and five-year returns stand at 15.1% and 13.5%, respectively. These figures are impressive when juxtaposed against the S&P 500, which returned 15.0% and 11.2% during the same periods. 

### Volatility and Sharpe Ratio

The fund's volatility, measured by standard deviation, is approximately 17.8%, higher than the S&P 500's 15.6%. However, the Sharpe ratio of 0.85 indicates that the excess return per unit of risk is commendable. Investors should note that while higher volatility can imply greater risk, it also offers the potential for higher returns in a bullish market.

### Fee Analysis

The total expense ratio (TER) of Fidelity Contrafund is 0.82%, which is relatively competitive within the actively managed fund space. In contrast, many index funds operate at a much lower fee structure averaging around 0.20%. While the higher fees associated with active management can be justified by potential outperformance, investors must analyze whether the additional costs translate into superior returns.
