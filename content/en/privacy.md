## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 12, 2026**

At **NovumWorld**, we value your privacy as much as decentralization. This policy transparently explains how we treat any data that interacts with our website.

### 1. Data Collection

We do not require registration to read our content. Browsing NovumWorld is free and open. However, we collect certain automatic technical data to ensure the site's operation:
*   **Server Logs:** IP address, browser type, date and time of access.
*   **Technical Cookies:** Essential for secure navigation.

### 2. Google AdSense and Third-Party Cookies

We use **Google AdSense** to monetize our content and maintain editorial independence.
*   Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites.
*   You can opt out of personalized advertising in [Google Ad Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
*   These third-party cookies may track your browsing behavior to offer relevant advertising. NovumWorld has no direct control over these cookies.

### 3. Web Analytics

We may use analysis tools (such as Google Analytics or privacy-focused alternatives) to understand which articles are most read. This data is anonymized and used solely to improve our editorial strategy. We do not sell your browsing history to anyone.

### 4. User Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

We comply with international data protection regulations. You have the right to:
*   Request access to any personal data we hold (if any).
*   Request the deletion of your data ("Right to be forgotten").
*   Object to the processing of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at `legal@novumworld.com`.

### 5. External Links

Our articles contain links to external sources, scientific papers, and tools. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We recommend reading their policies if you decide to visit them.

### 6. Changes to Policy

We reserve the right to modify this policy to adapt to new technologies or legal requirements. Any changes will be published on this page with the corresponding update date.
