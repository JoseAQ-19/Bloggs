## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 2026**

At **NovumWorld**, we value your privacy.

### 1. Data Collection
We do not require registration to read our content. Browsing is free. We collect minimal server logs for security.

### 2. Cookies & AdSense
We use **Google AdSense**. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your visit. You can opt out in Google Ad Settings.

### 3. User Rights (GDPR)
You have the right to access or delete your data. Contact: `legal@novumworld.com`.
