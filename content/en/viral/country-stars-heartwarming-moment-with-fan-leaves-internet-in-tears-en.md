Moreover, the prevalence of AI-generated tracks could lead to a homogenization of music, where formulas replace genuine artistry. Kassie Jordan, of the singing duo Blue Honey, notes the formulaic nature of some modern country music, making it easier for AI to replicate. "The lyrics aren't as deep as they used to be," she states, highlighting a growing concern that the emotional depth that fans seek is being sacrificed for algorithmic efficiency.
