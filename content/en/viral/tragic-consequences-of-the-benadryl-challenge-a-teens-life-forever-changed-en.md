The Benadryl Challenge illustrates a broader societal issue: the misbelief that over-the-counter medications are inherently safe. Many young people underestimate the potential for abuse and severe side effects, viewing drugs like Benadryl as benign. Johnson & Johnson, the manufacturer of Benadryl, has issued numerous warnings regarding the misuse of their products. Despite these warnings, a significant number of teenagers remain unaware of the dangers associated with taking large doses of such medications. 

For instance, Benadryl contains diphenhydramine, which can cause severe respiratory issues, heart problems, and even death when taken in excessive amounts. The reality is that many teens fail to recognize that "just because you can buy it without a prescription doesn't mean it's safe to misuse." In this sense, the challenge not only endangers the participants but also perpetuates a dangerous narrative that normalizes substance misuse under the guise of fun and entertainment.
