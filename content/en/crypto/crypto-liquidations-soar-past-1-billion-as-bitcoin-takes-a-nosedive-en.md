However, the recent downturn contradicts this narrative, exposing the risks associated with Bitcoin as an investment vehicle.

Key data point: Historical data shows that Bitcoin's volatility can lead to significant investor losses. For instance, during the 2021 bull run, Bitcoin saw massive price fluctuations, with its value dropping over 50% at various points throughout the year. Such volatility undermines the narrative of Bitcoin as a stable store of value, raising concerns about its long-term viability as a safe haven asset.

Moreover, the perception of Bitcoin as a hedge against inflation is increasingly challenged by its correlation with risk assets in traditional markets. As macroeconomic factors such as interest rate hikes and geopolitical tensions influence investor sentiment, Bitcoin's price movements often mirror those of equities rather than displaying the independence one might expect from an inflation hedge. The narrative of resilience appears flawed when framed within the reality of market dynamics and investor behavior.
