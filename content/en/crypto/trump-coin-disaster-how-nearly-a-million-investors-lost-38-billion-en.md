## The $3.8 Billion Disaster: Trump’s Memecoin Meltdown

This catastrophic event serves as a critical reminder of the volatility inherent in the cryptocurrency landscape, especially within the realm of celebrity-endorsed tokens. Specifically, Nansen reported that 988,905 accounts have suffered losses, indicating that two out of every three investors who purchased the $TRUMP token lost their entire investment.

This sharp depreciation is not merely an isolated incident but rather a culmination of the broader trends in the memecoin market. The $TRUMP token, once considered a novelty, experienced a meteoric rise to an all-time high of $75.35 before plummeting to just $1.69. This 98% drop underscores the fragility of assets that heavily rely on celebrity endorsements and speculative trading.
